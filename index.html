the ai agents i run my own job search on. each one drafts; i decide. nothing is sent or saved without my approval.

problem

gcc roles are spread across linkedin, bayt, gulftalent and dozens of custom career pages. checking them by hand is slow, and the best roles fill fast.

how it works

  • searches each source and every target company's career page
  • scores each role on role type × company fit × freshness
  • returns a ranked digest with a direct link to every posting, and only adds rows i approve to my tracker

design choice

review-first: a tracker full of half-relevant roles is worse than no tracker.
